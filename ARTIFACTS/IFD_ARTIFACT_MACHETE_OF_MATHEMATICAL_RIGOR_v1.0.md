## Canonical name

## Inscription

> **SHOW YOUR WORK, MOTHERFUCKER.**

## Description

A symbolic tool for cutting through unsupported assumptions, invalid inference, mathematical errors, and claims that outrun their evidence. It is used in the Cathedral’s research expedition as an emblem of rigorous, step-by-step examination.

The Machete must cut in both directions: it scrutinizes claims we distrust and tests our own preferred interpretations. If an unorthodox derivation is valid, the blade clears the path for it; if a step fails, the failure must be identified precisely.

## Operating principles

1. Follow the mathematics.
2. Check every step of the derivation.
3. Distinguish established mathematics from philosophical or ontological interpretation.
4. Mark assumptions and logical leaps rather than silently filling gaps.
5. Preserve valid results even when broader conclusions fail.
6. Apply the same standard to our own ideas and to ideas we oppose.
7. Do not confuse a beautiful presentation, an author's reputation, or an unconventional appearance with evidence for or against a claim.
