## Purpose and rules

This is the single index for IFD names deliberately styled with ™ and related candidate marks. It points to individual artifact records; it does not reproduce them.

The register distinguishes:
- **Coined / identified:** the name is present in a conversation or source record.
- **Captured:** entered in this register.
- **Artifact archived:** a supporting artifact exists at the stated location.
- **Location verified:** the actual destination was inspected during this register's creation.
- **Source verification pending:** earliest origin, exact date, or full context has not yet been recovered.

The ™ symbol records trademark-style designation in the IFD working vocabulary. It does **not** establish legal registration, availability, exclusivity, ownership, or legal clearance. No legal clearance search is represented here.
