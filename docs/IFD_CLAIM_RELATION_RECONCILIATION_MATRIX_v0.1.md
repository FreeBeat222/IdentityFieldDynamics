## 1. Reading rules

This register separates:
- **Claim status:** what kind of proposition IFD is making and its current standing.
- **Review status:** how far the source and reasoning have actually been inspected.
- **Relation type:** what a connection between two terms means.
- **Evidence:** what the cited source actually supports, not what a diagram might imply.

The row IDs below are working identifiers for this register. They are not canonical CODEX IDs and do not replace existing CODEX, FLAW CHART™, or asset identifiers.

A source citation establishes provenance for the wording or decision; it does not automatically establish the proposition's truth.

## 4. Status and review controls

Use the CODEX vocabulary for knowledge status:
- CANONICAL PRINCIPLE
- FORMALIZED
- PROVISIONAL MODEL
- SYMBOLIC / INTERPRETIVE
- EMPIRICALLY VALIDATED
- OPEN / UNRESOLVED
- HISTORICAL / SUPERSEDED

Record review status separately:
- NOT INSPECTED
- SOURCE LOCATED
- CONTENT INSPECTED
- SOURCE-COMPARED
- RELATION-RECONCILED
- FORMALLY DERIVED
- EMPIRICALLY TESTED
- VALIDATED WITHIN STATED SCOPE

A claim may be canonical as a declared IFD commitment while remaining untested as an empirical proposition. A mathematical relationship may be formally established while its IFD interpretation remains provisional. A visual exhibit may be correctly rendered while proving only the mathematical relationship it actually depicts.
