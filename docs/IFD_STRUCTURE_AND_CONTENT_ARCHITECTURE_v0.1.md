## 1. Purpose

Establish one clean, explicit architecture for presenting and developing Identity Field Dynamics (IFD) on the IFD website without discarding or prematurely canonizing the accumulated corpus.

This specification organizes existing material. It does not claim that every listed concept is true, formalized, causally connected, or empirically validated.

**Governing principle:** Preserve the whole. Increase the resolution. Exercise restraint.

**Development sequence:** Harvest first. Abstract second. Canonize last.

## 2. The five-domain architecture

The public and research architecture is organized into five mutually connected domains:

1. **STRUCTURE** — what the discipline investigates; its entities, distinctions, boundaries, levels, and components.
2. **LOGIC** — what relations are asserted between components; the type and direction of each relation; premises, implications, constraints, and contradictions.
3. **FORMULATION** — the current integrated representation of IFD, including definitions, models, mathematical expressions, mechanisms under investigation, and open hypotheses.
4. **EXHIBITS** — inspectable demonstrations and source material: observations, experiments, datasets, derivations, diagrams, instruments, case records, and research outputs.
5. **GOVERNANCE & PROVENANCE** — the status, origin, authority, version, evidence, and revision history of every material claim and artifact.

The first four are the requested intellectual/public domains. Governance and provenance are the control layer that keeps them honest.

These domains are not a linear theory sequence. They are coordinated views of one developing body of knowledge.

## 3. Distinguish the discipline from its representation

- **IFD** is the discipline and research program.
- **The Current IFD Formulation** is the present integrated working representation of the discipline's account of identity.
- **The website** is a public interface and navigable projection of selected IFD records.

A website diagram is not the phenomenon. A formulation is not reality itself. A registered asset is not validated knowledge. A linked exhibit does not automatically validate every claim in the page that links to it.

## 5. LOGIC — relation typing is mandatory

Every meaningful connection in the formulation must be assigned a relation type. Do not use an unqualified arrow when its meaning matters.

Permitted relation labels include:

- **Temporal succession:** A occurs before B.
- **Constitutive:** A is part of, or helps define, B.
- **Functional:** A performs a specified role relative to B.
- **Informational:** A carries, records, or changes information relevant to B.
- **Dependency:** B requires A under stated conditions.
- **Causal hypothesis:** changing A is predicted to change B through a proposed mechanism.
- **Observational:** A provides evidence about B without necessarily causing it.
- **Mathematical equivalence / derivation:** an exact relation established under stated definitions and assumptions.
- **Analogy:** a comparison that aids understanding but does not establish identity of mechanism.
- **Symbolic / interpretive association:** a meaning-bearing relation not asserted as an empirical mechanism.
- **Open / unspecified:** the relationship has not yet been determined.

Each edge in a diagram or prose formulation should be understood as carrying a relation type, even when the display uses a simple arrow for readability.

## 7. EXHIBITS — evidence must be inspectable

Exhibits are the bridge between formulation and scrutiny. Each exhibit must identify what it demonstrates and what it does not demonstrate.

- **Instrument exhibits:** OBSERVOR, Quiz Lab, FLAW CHART™, and their protocols, versions, limitations, and outputs.
- **Structural exhibits:** diagrams, dependency maps, relation graphs, state/phase maps.
- **Case exhibits:** bounded examples with context, source, selection criteria, and alternative interpretations.
- **Historical exhibits:** source passages, dated decisions, intellectual development, and provenance.
- **Demonstration exhibits:** interactive or executable demonstrations, with explicit separation between illustration and evidence.

### 7.2 Exhibit record schema

Every exhibit should carry:

A diagram illustrates a proposed relationship. It is not empirical evidence merely because it is visually persuasive.

## 8. OBSERVOR — active v2 method boundary

OBSERVOR 2.0 is the only active OBSERVOR instrument/method track.

Its governing approach is phenomenon-first:
1. measure observable behavior;
2. identify a candidate property;
3. manipulate or ablate that property;
4. compare matched conditions and repetitions;
5. test information-equivalent and sham controls;
6. evaluate whether a candidate construct is warranted only after the preceding evidence.

Conditions:
- H+ — candidate history/property retained;
- H− — candidate history/property ablated;
- H−I — candidate history removed internally but equivalent information supplied externally;
- SHAM — procedure without removal of the candidate causal property.

Interpretation ladder:
- L0 observable behavior;
- L1 reproducible pattern;
- L2 persistent/distinctive organization;
- L3 causal historical dependence;
- L4 candidate construct, if warranted.

OBSERVOR does not calculate an identity score. No identity declaration, self-report, consciousness, intelligence, language, or legacy W/V/B/A/N/D variables are prerequisites.

Do not import OBSERVOR v5 variables, scoring systems, or analyses into v2. The active repository implementation and method must be evaluated on their own source and records.

**Current implementation limitation:** The inspected v2 HTML tool records trials in browser memory and exports JSON on user request. Its visible comparison summary is descriptive and does not itself establish matched design, randomization, sufficient repetitions, causal identification, or validated inference. The existing Playwright tests are smoke tests, not scientific validation. Underlying experimental records and their provenance remain to be located and checked before reproducing any earlier analysis.

## 9. Four-channel workflow — operating architecture

The Cathedral's four channels govern how IFD work is carried out:

1. **RESEARCH:** gather source material, observations, questions, and candidate relationships.
2. **CONSTRUCTION:** define, model, formulate, diagram, and build instruments.
3. **VERIFICATION:** check logic, derivations, evidence, implementation, and counterexamples.
4. **ARCHIVING:** preserve sources, versions, decisions, findings, and unresolved questions.

These are coordinated functions operating on one shared knowledge substrate, not four separate versions of the discipline. Work can move between channels in either direction.

Governing conditions:
- HOME-BASED;
- BOOTSTRAPPED;
- NECESSITY-DRIVEN CONSTRUCTION.

Governing axiom:
**DESIGN FOR THE WHOLE. BUILD FOR THE NEED.**

The architecture may be comprehensive; implementation remains incremental and justified by demonstrated need.

## 12. Page acceptance standard

A public page is ready for publication review when:
- its purpose and place in the architecture are explicit;
- its key terms have stable definitions or are visibly marked open;
- every substantive claim has a status;
- relationships are typed or explicitly provisional;
- mathematical expressions have definitions and assumptions;
- evidence is linked and accurately characterized;
- limitations and open questions are visible;
- the page links to its parent formulation and relevant exhibits;
- it does not duplicate or contradict a controlling definition without explanation;
- the page has been checked for readability, responsive layout, and working links.

## 13. Development sequence

Proceed in bounded, evidence-preserving increments:

Do not rewrite all pages at once. Prefer one complete, useful, reversible improvement at a time.
