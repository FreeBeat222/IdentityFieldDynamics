- **Expanded name:** Failure Logging, Analysis, and Verification
- **Category:** IFD research instrument / defect-tracking protocol
- **First recorded in this register:** 2026-10-30
- **Canonical specification:** `docs/FLAW_CHART_SPEC_v1.0.md`

- **Role:** Identify, register, classify, investigate, track, verify, and document suspected and demonstrated weaknesses in IFD formulations, models, methods, instruments, and implementations.
- **Usage note:** Retain the ™ symbol in the public-facing name and first prominent mention. The symbol indicates a claimed trademark-style designation; it does not by itself establish registration or legal exclusivity.
- **Status:** Named working instrument; operational performance remains subject to testing and review.

### Governing distinction

A suspected weakness is not a demonstrated defect. A proposed correction is not a verified correction. The instrument itself remains open to scrutiny.
