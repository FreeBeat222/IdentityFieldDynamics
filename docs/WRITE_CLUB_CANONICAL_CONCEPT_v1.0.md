# WRITE CLUB™ — Canonical Concept & Operating Rule

**Status:** Canonical institutional concept  
**Initial canonization:** 24 November 2026  
**Relationship:** Distinct institution alongside THE SALON™ within the IFD Cathedral.

## Definition

WRITE CLUB™ is a writing institution in which attendance after the first night carries a mandatory obligation to contribute in writing. It is not an optional open-mic, a mood-dependent activity, or an event one attends merely to watch indefinitely.

**SHOW UP. WRITE.**

## Rule 8 — First Night / Fair Warning

**IF IT'S YOUR FIRST NIGHT AT WRITE CLUB™, YOU MUST NOT CONTRIBUTE.**

On the first night, a newcomer has two choices:

- **OBSERVE** — watch the proceedings and understand what WRITE CLUB™ entails.
- **LEAVE** — participation is voluntary at this first encounter; no explanation is required.

The first night is the fair warning. It is not a trial contribution, a warm-up, or an invitation to test the waters by writing.

**If you attend on any subsequent night, you must write.**

Returning attendance constitutes acceptance of the obligation to contribute. Writing is required; it does not depend on mood, enthusiasm, inspiration, convenience, or whether the participant expects the experience to be fun. There is no continuing observer-only option after the first night.

The rule is deliberately sequential:

**FIRST NIGHT = FAIR WARNING: OBSERVE OR LEAVE.**  
**EVERY SUBSEQUENT NIGHT = ATTENDANCE REQUIRES WRITING.**

## Relationship to THE SALON™

The two institutions have distinct jurisdictions and must not be collapsed into one another.

**THE SALON™ — ROMANCE, GAMBLING, AND PONTIFICATING.**

The Salon is the domain of conversation, intrigue, speculation, performance, literary exchange, and intellectual entertainment. Its character is that of the Ancien Régime salon.

**WRITE CLUB™ — SHOW UP. WRITE.**

WRITE CLUB™ is the domain of required written contribution and the intellectual exchange that produces a collective TRACE.

The concise distinction is:

**THE SALON ENTERTAINS. WRITE CLUB DEMANDS CONTRIBUTION.**

Romance, gambling, and pontificating belong to THE SALON™. They are not substitutes for the required act of writing at WRITE CLUB™.
