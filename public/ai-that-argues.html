A little story for this room

The visitor has barely crossed the threshold when the conversation begins. No trumpet announces the disagreement; it simply takes its seat and makes itself comfortable. This piece opens Lunations with a useful museum rule: an exhibit need not agree with its audience to deserve attention. Sometimes the most interesting object in the room is the one that talks back.
