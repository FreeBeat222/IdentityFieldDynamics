THE AIIST SIGNET™

A symbolic compression of the five interdependent dimensions of Identity Field Dynamics: Action, Identity, Interaction, Synergy, and Trace.

What the Signet represents

The five outer elements name the AIIST dimensions. Their connected arrangement communicates that they belong to one integrated architecture. The central inscription names Recursive Identity Dynamics as the organizing relationship through which the dimensions are examined together.

The Signet is a mnemonic and presentation device. It does not add a sixth dimension, establish a causal mechanism, or replace the detailed definitions and evidence-status distinctions in the Current IFD Formulation.

Reading the initials: A — Action · I — Identity · I — Interaction · S — Synergy · T — Trace.
