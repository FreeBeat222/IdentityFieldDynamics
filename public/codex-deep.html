The Codex.

The Codex makes the evolving discipline inspectable without pretending unfinished material is already canonical.

Canon

Definitions, notation, claims, status, and historical provenance.

Mechanics

Coupling hypotheses, dynamic models, phase transitions, and systemic disequilibrium.

Validation

Experiments, observables, competing models, preregistration, results, and falsification.

Registry

Claims, constructs, variables, equations, experiments, and current status.

Provenance

Historical Identity Physics material remains identifiable rather than rewritten as though it began as IFD.

Revision

When evidence changes the model, the record shows what changed and why.
