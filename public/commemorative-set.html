Presentation & context

This is the final assembled composition selected and composed by Rick. It brings Luna-originated visual designs into a single presentation and includes the deliberately irreverent front-nameplate inscription.

The set is exhibited as a composition in its own right. It is not a replacement for the individual source designs.

Source, selection & assembly

The creative source, the human decisions of selection and arrangement, and the final assembled image are distinguishable stages. The original file is preserved separately.

The committee of one meets the design studio

A collection of separate designs can remain a collection—or it can be assembled into a statement. This set is the latter. Luna's visual designs supply the ingredients; Rick's selection and arrangement give the final composition its particular shape. The front-nameplate inscription ensures that ceremony does not become unbearable.
