The whole architecture: What, how, and why.

The formulation brings several engines and temporal dimensions into one proposed architecture. The diagram is deliberately a map of relationships, not a claim that every arrow has been proven as a physical causal mechanism. The components must be read together and also examined individually.

The architecture is inclusive: it places the proposed generative, temporal, imprinting, operational, and collective dimensions in one account. Inclusiveness is not proof of accuracy. The explanatory connections and mechanisms remain open to investigation.

Why integrate the components?

IFD is not satisfied by collecting related terms in a glossary. The aim is to determine whether the components can be aligned, whether their relationships can be made intelligible, and whether the integrated account remains answerable to manifestation and evidence. A whole-system account should make it possible to move from an originating proposition to its definitions, from definitions to proposed relationships, from relationships to research questions, and from research to documented results and limitations.
