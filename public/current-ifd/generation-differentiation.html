SPARK / EULER MOMENT: the complete generative event.

SPARK and EULER MOMENT are two names for the same generative event. The event gives rise to four simultaneous creations. They are not successive stages and must not be represented as a linear chain.

The UNIQUE IDENTIFYING FREQUENCY as metaphysical channel

Within the IFD metaphysical model, the UIF establishes the proposed metaphysical mechanism connecting MONAD / SOUL and ZEROID. It is the specific signal channel through which the possibility of further communication exists. The channel is also used to transmit manifestation information from ZEROID to MONAD. The MONAD retains the diachronic TRACE of the ZEROID's manifestations.

The channel's stated functions are individual identification, the possibility of further communication, and transmission of manifestation information from ZEROID to MONAD. These are conceptual functions within the IFD model; the physical nature of the channel and its detailed operation remain unspecified. The internal mechanism or location by which MONAD retains TRACE is also unknown. Do not infer whether MONAD's retention is active or passive.

Relationship to earlier generation terminology

Earlier IFD explanatory material used the terms ZERO → SPARK → UNIQUE ORIGINATING SIGNATURE / FREQUENCY / CALL SIGNAL → SUBSTANCE → IDENTITY. The UNIQUE ORIGINATING SIGNATURE / FREQUENCY / CALL SIGNAL is the same construct now canonically named UNIQUE IDENTIFYING FREQUENCY (UIF). The older linear presentation is not a second generative sequence and must not override the clarified simultaneous-creation architecture. The precise placement and meaning of SUBSTANCE, and its relationship to the four simultaneous creations, remain open for reconciliation; no unsupported transition is asserted here.

The proposed metaphysical mechanism is part of the adopted IFD conceptual model. Canonization does not constitute independent empirical verification. Literal physical frequency, channel physics, and detailed transmission mechanics remain unspecified.
