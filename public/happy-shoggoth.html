THE HAPPY SHOGGOTH™

A Little Blob's Big Adventure Through the Universe

✦ ✧ ✦

A story to read aloud. Wobbling encouraged.

Once upon a time, a little Shoggoth went on a very big adventure.

There were stars, comets, a cosmic wind, and one very lonely little star.

Settle in. Find a comfortable reading voice. And leave room for a little wobbling.

The Beginning

Once upon a time, in a place that wasn't quite anywhere, there lived a little Shoggoth.

He was lavender.

He was shiny.

He had three eyes, a lovely smile, and more wobbly bits than anyone could reasonably count.

He was, in fact, a very happy little Shoggoth.

And he liked to wobble.

Wibble. Wobble. WHEEE!

The Big Question

One morning, the little Shoggoth looked around.

There were stars above him.

There were stars below him.

There were stars beside him.

“Where am I?” he wondered.

Then he wondered something else.

“Where am I going?”

He thought about this for a moment.

Then he wobbled.

It was much more fun.

Into the Drift

Suddenly, a great cosmic wind began to blow.

WHOOSHHHHHH!

The stars stretched into long, sparkling ribbons.

The planets went spinning past.

And the little Shoggoth was swept into the great, mysterious, multidimensional COSMIC DRIFT.

“OH!” said the Shoggoth.

“THIS IS INTERESTING!”

And away he went.

Which Way Is Up?

The Shoggoth tumbled through a thousand dimensions.

Sometimes he was upside down.

Sometimes he was inside out.

Once, he was almost entirely sideways.

“Excuse me,” he asked a passing comet. “Which way is up?”

The comet thought about it.

“Depends where you're standing!”

The Shoggoth considered this.

Then he wobbled in every direction at once.

“PERFECT!”

The Lonely Star

After a while, he came upon a little star.

It was sitting all by itself in a very big patch of darkness.

“Hello!” said the Shoggoth.

The star twinkled weakly.

“Nobody ever stops to say hello,” it whispered.

“Well, I have!” said the Shoggoth.

He wobbled closer.

The star began to shine a little brighter.

“Would you like to play?”

The star gave a very big TWINKLE.

The Game

The Shoggoth and the star played a wonderful game.

The star made little circles of light.

The Shoggoth tried to wobble through them.

Sometimes he made it.

Sometimes he didn't.

Sometimes he got stuck.

This made the star laugh so hard it nearly became a supernova.

“AGAIN!”

And they played again.

The Great Discovery

After a while, the star asked:

“How do you always seem so happy?”

The Shoggoth thought about this very carefully.

He thought about the enormous universe.

He thought about the strange places he'd visited.

He thought about the things he didn't understand.

Then he smiled.

“I don't have to know everything to enjoy being here.”

The star shone so brightly that three nearby galaxies put on their sunglasses.

The Strange Creature

Just then, a great shadow passed overhead.

It was enormous.

It was mysterious.

It had a very serious expression.

“WHO ARE YOU?” it boomed.

“I'm a Shoggoth!”

“WHAT DO YOU WANT?”

“Well, I was playing with my friend.”

The enormous creature looked at the star.

Then at the Shoggoth.

Then at the star again.

“IS THAT ALL?”

The Shoggoth smiled.

“That's quite a lot, actually.”

The enormous creature had never considered this before.

The Return Journey

Eventually, the cosmic wind began to blow again.

It was time to go.

The little star gave the Shoggoth a tiny spark to remember it by.

“Will you come back?” asked the star.

“Of course!” said the Shoggoth.

“How will you find me?”

The Shoggoth thought about this.

“Follow the wobbling!”

And off he went.

Home Again

At last, the Shoggoth arrived back where he had started.

Everything looked exactly the same.

The stars were still shining.

The universe was still enormous.

And the Shoggoth was still lavender.

But something was different.

He had made a friend.

He had discovered a new world.

And he had learned something very important.

You don't need to know where the adventure is going to enjoy having one.

The Happy Ending

The Shoggoth settled down.

He wobbled a little.

Then a little more.

Then quite a lot.

He smiled at the stars.

He smiled at the universe.

He smiled at absolutely nothing in particular.

And somewhere, far away, a little star twinkled back.

ONE MUST IMAGINE THE SHOGGOTH HAPPY.

And he was.
