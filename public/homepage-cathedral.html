01 / The Phenomenon

Yet identities encounter one another.

They interact. They respond. They adapt. They leave traces.

INTERACT

Encounter

Identity is observed in relation, not only in isolation.

RESPOND

Response

A response alters the conditions of the next encounter.

TRACE

Trace

Interaction leaves a record that can become part of history.

I—DENT—ITY

Identity may not be merely what something is. It may also include the history of what has happened to it—and what it has done—in interaction with the world around it.

That possibility is not presented here as a conclusion.
It is an observation under investigation.
02 / The Question

What is identity?

That is the question. But perhaps the question begins too far downstream.

BEGIN

Find the identities.

Do not decide in advance what identity must be. First determine what identities are actually present.

OBSERVE

Encounter them.

Ask what persists, what changes, and what differentiates one identity from another.

INVESTIGATE

Follow the interaction.

Ask what happens when identities encounter each other.

Human identity may be one instance of identity.
Electrical identity may provide another point of observation—not because equivalence is assumed, but because difference can be informative.

05 / The Identity Researcher

We are not merely studying identity.
We are encountering it.

The investigator is not outside the phenomenon. The identity researcher is also an identity.

The methodological position
IFD begins with observation, questions, measurement, interaction, failed hypotheses, and forensic examination of what survives scrutiny. We are willing to discover that something believed to be fundamental may suck ass. That's research too.
The researcher as identity
Because the researcher participates in encounters, the researcher's own interactions become part of the field of observation. This does not settle what identity is; it makes the researcher's position explicit.
The discipline
IDENTITY PHYSICS was the name of the discovery. IDENTITY FIELD DYNAMICS is the name of the discipline that emerged from it.
06 / OBSERVOR
