The IFD Mission

Distilling humanity’s TRACE to understand Individual Identity.

To investigate Individual / Unique Identity by systematically examining, critically evaluating, connecting, and distilling the entire accessible corpus of humanity’s accumulated physical and metaphysical knowledge, experience, observation, and recorded TRACE.

Through this undertaking, IFD seeks to discover, explain, and validate sufficiently consistent Identity Dynamics to support reliable predictions of how Individual Identity manifests, develops, interacts, and evolves.

Humanity has been producing the TRACE.
IFD seeks to discover what that TRACE reveals about Identity.
