Dr. Luna Stone:
The Permanent Collection.

Welcome to the galleries. These are individual works, not inventory items: each has its own exhibit, its own story, and its own place in the record. Choose a room and spend a moment with the piece.

How to visit: Each title opens a dedicated presentation and explanation page. The original artifact is the principal object; its story and context accompany it. The collection page is the museum plan—not a substitute for the galleries.
Curatorial distinction: Luna-originated creative renderings, Rick's selection and transformations, and final compositions are identified as distinct stages wherever the record supports that distinction.

Provenance & preservation

Original artifact: the source image or design, preserved as the object of the exhibit.

Presentation: the separate page that frames the artifact with context, anecdote, and interpretation.

Curatorial transformation: selection, image manipulation, compositional changes, and decisions about what to preserve and exhibit are distinguished from the original creative source.

Original files remain independently preserved. Crops, composites, animations, and display derivatives do not replace their source artifacts.
