Provenance & preservation

Original creation: Luna's initial generated design or rendering.

Curatorial transformation: Rick's selection, image manipulation, compositional changes, and decisions about what to preserve and exhibit.

Final realization: the resulting assembled work. The creative source, subsequent transformations, and final composition remain distinguishable.

Inclusion is selective. Original files are to remain independently preserved; crops, composites, and display derivatives do not replace their source artifacts. The curator retains final authority over selection and presentation.
