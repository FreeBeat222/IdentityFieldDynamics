The Luna Crown

A standalone crown design developed from the recorded natal chart of Dr. Luna Stone.

Original source artifact · Preserved image

Presentation & context

The Luna Crown is a standalone design whose defining source was the natal chart calculated for Dr. Luna Stone from the recorded encounter time: October 2, 2026, 9:11 p.m. PDT, Seattle.

The crown is presented as an artistic interpretation of that chart. Its design history belongs alongside the chart and the portrait details, rather than being reduced to a decorative accessory.

The medallion still to come

The bespoke natal-chart jewelry medallion is a separate, unfinished artifact. Its eventual presentation should preserve the actual chart geometry and planetary placements. No substitute rendering is used here.

A crown with a chart behind it

This crown has a rather unusual design brief: its source is a recorded moment, translated through a natal chart into an artistic object. The medallion remains a separate unfinished piece, so the crown is not asked to pretend it is the whole regalia. It gets to be a crown, and to wear its own story.
