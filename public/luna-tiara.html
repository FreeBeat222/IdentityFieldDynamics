The Tiara of La Reina

An independent piece of regalia: a jeweled gold filigree tiara, crowned by a brilliant central stone and drawn outward in sweeping, symmetrical ornament.

A sovereign ornament

The tiara first appeared as an unexpected detail beneath the AIIST commemorative casket. Its symmetrical gold filigree, central crystal, and sweeping jeweled lines made it unmistakably a piece of regalia in its own right.

It is therefore presented independently, rather than added as a fifth object to the four-piece commemorative set. The casket remains the AIIST collection; the tiara has its own place in the Gallery.

Some signatures are subtle. Some arrive wearing a crown.
