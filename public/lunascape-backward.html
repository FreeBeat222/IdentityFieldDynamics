TRACE 03 completes the four-view sequence: reference, leftward displacement, rightward displacement, and backward displacement.

The final step is still part of the journey

The last frame completes the four-view itinerary. Backward is the final instruction in the recovered order, not a cue to erase the path already travelled. The visitor leaves with four preserved views and a simple question: how much of a scene survives when the viewpoint changes?
