The Fifth Element · Motion Integration Film

Four views. One integrated TRACE.

The Lunascape began as four original still images generated in response to successive viewpoint instructions: Reference, Leftward, Rightward, and Backward. Each remains an independent source artifact, preserved in its own exhibit.

The Fifth Element is their motion integration: the four perspectives enter temporal relationship and are experienced as one continuous sequence. It does not replace, modify, or subsume the four source records. It makes their ordered relationship visible in motion.

How the five-part collection is read

Enter the four source exhibits individually to examine each preserved viewpoint. Then return here for the integrated film. The stills are the component records; the film is the temporal composition that brings them together.

The Fifth Element: integration without erasure

Four views remain four. Their motion-integrated presentation becomes the fifth element—not a fifth viewpoint, but a new way to encounter the relationship among the original four. The film points back to its source exhibits; the source exhibits give the film its substance.

The film is an animated presentation assembled from the four preserved stills. The original artifacts remain independently accessible and unchanged.
