Measurement Infrastructure

OBSERVOR

OBSERVOR is the developing measurement and discovery engine supporting the IFD empirical program.

Missions

Structured tasks designed to produce observable behavior relevant to specific research questions.

Event capture

Timestamped actions and system events form the durable substrate for analysis.

Data lineage

Observations remain traceable to session, trial, event, and derivation.

Integrity

Recovery, hashing, manifests, and validation protect the research record.

Operationalization

Candidate constructs connect to observable behavior without premature canonical scores.

Discovery

The instrument generates evidence for model development rather than manufacturing confirmation.
