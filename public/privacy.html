Statement of Privacy

EFFECTIVE DATE: 28 OCTOBER 2026
This notice explains the information practices identifiable from the website source reviewed for publication. Some hosting-level and operational details have not been independently verified; those limits are stated below rather than assumed.

Identity Field Dynamics (IFD) is a developing research project. This notice describes source-code behavior identified during a review on 28 October 2026. Source code alone cannot establish every behavior of the deployed website, its hosting environment, or third-party services.

1. Scope

This notice covers the public IFD website and its Research Intake. Different interactive tools may handle information differently. The observations below are based on source review and do not establish every behavior of the deployed pages, infrastructure, or services.

2. Research Intake submissions

In the source version reviewed, submitting the Research Intake sends the submitted fields to the IFD server. The application writes structured fields to a MySQL research_intake table and attempts to send the record to the configured research mailbox. An email delivery failure may occur even if the database record has been saved. The application creates an intake identifier.

Fields may include encounter type and participant categories, optional participant details, event summary and sequence, approximate first-observed date/time, observable behavior, persistence and repeatability, evidence and independent trace details, the submitter's interpretation and research question, optional name and email, follow-up preference, and additional notes.

Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information about yourself or others unless necessary, appropriate, and authorized. Where possible, describe people or systems without identifying them.

The follow-up preference concerns whether the submitter may be contacted; it does not determine whether a submission is stored or routed for processing.

Operational limitation: the source reviewed describes the application behavior; the live deployed handler and all hosting-level data flows have not been independently verified.

3. Purpose of collection

The reviewed source indicates that Research Intake submissions are collected to receive and review research observations and questions, preserve the submitted record, and route it to the research mailbox. Any additional purposes must be identified before the final policy is approved.

4. Interactive tools and browser-side information

In the page scripts reviewed to date, Quiz Lab and OBSERVOR interaction results appeared to remain in page memory, with an OBSERVOR user-triggered local JSON export. A reviewed /lab prototype records a choice and response time in the active page script. No server submission was found in those specific reviewed scripts.

A separate telemetry development branch contains code for page-view and selected interaction events sent to /api/telemetry, with an ephemeral per-tab session identifier. Its deployment and merge status have not been established in this review. The final policy must be based on the actual deployed version and live network behavior.

These are limited source-review observations, not a guarantee about every page, browser, extension, hosting component, or future version. Cookies, local storage, third-party scripts, and other transfers require a full deployed-page and browser-network audit.

5. Recipients and service providers

The reviewed source uses a configured MySQL database and configured mail relay/recipient to process Research Intake submissions. Hosting providers, database services, email providers, authorized administrators, and their subcontractors may process information or technical data as part of providing those services.

The exact hosting, database, and email providers, their service settings, personnel access, and processing locations have not been independently confirmed for this notice. Source-code defaults are not proof of the providers currently in use.

6. Technical and server information

The application source reviewed did not show an application-level analytics package, general request logger, or cookie/session system in that particular server version. This does not establish whether the hosting provider, proxy, firewall, CDN, server runtime, database, or email infrastructure records IP addresses, timestamps, user-agent strings, security events, or other technical information.

The separate telemetry branch is an additional reason to verify which version is deployed before describing analytics or logging practices.

7. Storage, security, and retention

In the source version reviewed, Research Intake records are stored in the configured database, and email copies may exist in the research mailbox. The application source does not define a deletion schedule or automatic retention limit. Database backups, mailbox retention, access controls, encryption at rest, provider backups, and deletion processes have not been verified.

No specific retention period, deletion guarantee, confidentiality guarantee, or absolute-security promise is made by this notice. The actual retention approach, access restrictions, backup behavior, incident response, and deletion process have not been independently verified.

The reviewed application disables Express's X-Powered-By header and disables public lookup of intake records by intake ID. Those measures do not establish that all information is confidential or eliminate all security risk.

8. Choices and privacy requests

The reviewed intake form permits a submitter to leave name and email blank and choose not to permit follow-up contact. Those choices do not prevent processing needed to accept, store, and route a research submission.

The site does not currently provide a dedicated privacy-request channel: /contact redirects to the Research Intake form. Do not include sensitive personal information in that form for a privacy request. Whether access, correction, deletion, objection, or other rights apply depends on applicable law and circumstances; this notice does not promise an unimplemented capability.

9. Children and external links

The site does not state a verified age-specific collection or consent procedure. External research resources linked from the site are governed by their own operators' practices. The source review found no third-party embedded analytics scripts in the specific pages checked, but that is not a guarantee for every deployed resource.

10. Changes and contact

This notice is effective 28 October 2026 and may be updated when data flows, providers, or practices materially change. The current /contact route redirects to the Research Intake form; it is not a dedicated privacy-request channel.

Known limits of this notice

  • Hosting-level logs, database and mailbox retention, backups, access controls, and deletion behavior have not been independently verified.
  • The deployed version and all third-party/browser data transfers have not been independently audited end to end.
  • The source reviewed includes a raw_payload database field; whether historical records contain duplicate or additional submitted data requires operational inspection.
  • No dedicated monitored privacy-request channel is currently identified on the site.

This notice states these limitations openly and does not promise controls or capabilities that have not been verified. It is effective 28 October 2026 and should be updated when operational facts are confirmed or practices change.
