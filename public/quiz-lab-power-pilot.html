POWER:
WHO SETS THE CHOICES?

Power may be visible in a command. It may also be present in the quiet ability to decide which options exist, which costs attach to them, and who must live with the consequences.

Five situations. No personality score. Watch what the available choices reveal—and what they do not.

Research in development. This is an exploratory reflection, not a validated measurement instrument. Your selections are processed in this page only; nothing is submitted or stored. A selected response does not establish what you would do in real circumstances.

The question is an invitation.

Power is treated here as a question to investigate—not a settled definition. Look for observable constraints, who can change them, who bears the cost, and what evidence would change your interpretation.

This pilot does not diagnose people or presume that any one response is morally correct. Circumstances matter; consequences matter; explanations remain open to examination.
