THE ROADBUILDER QUESTION

Who constructs the choices available to other identities? A system may shape decisions without explicitly forcing anyone. To investigate that possibility, look not only at the intentions it announces, but at the options it makes available.

Five roadbuilding scenarios. Choose an interpretation, then choose what evidence you would examine next.

Research in development. Research in development. This exploratory exercise distinguishes interpretation from evidence-seeking and reflection. It is not a validated measurement instrument. Your selections are processed in this page only; nothing is submitted or stored. A selected response does not establish what you would do in real circumstances.

Examine the road, not only the announcement.

POWER is one of IFD’s Twelve Conceptual Triggers: “What shapes the road?” Its research question is whether the structure of available choices can reveal something about power, including influence exercised without explicit force.

There is no hidden personality type here. Separate what you observed, what you inferred, and what evidence could change your mind. The purpose is to generate questions—not to reward agreement with IFD.
