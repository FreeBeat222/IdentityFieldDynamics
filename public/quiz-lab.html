Real people make promises. Real people break them. Real people explain themselves. The hard part is deciding what the evidence actually means—including when the person in the mirror is you.

The situation is the mirror.

Identity Field Dynamics asks us to distinguish observation from inference, action from intention, and evidence from the story we tell about it. The goal is not to condemn a person. It is to make a better-supported judgment.
