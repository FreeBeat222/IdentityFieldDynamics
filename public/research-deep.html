03 / Research

Observation before conclusion.

IFD is a developing research program. Proposed constructs become research questions when they can be operationalized, observed, perturbed, compared against alternatives, and revised when evidence requires it.

Operationalize

Define observable variables without treating proposed constructs as settled measurements.

Observe

Capture structured behavior and decision patterns through durable trials.

Perturb

Introduce controlled changes and record system response.

Compare

Test competing specifications using prediction, fit, and cross-validation.

Falsify

State conditions under which a construct, coupling, or model would fail.

Revise

Preserve provenance, uncertainty, failures, and changes rather than rewriting history.
