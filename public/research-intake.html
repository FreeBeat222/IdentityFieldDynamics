HOW CAN
WE HELP?

If you've encountered something you think belongs in the investigation, don't simply send us an email. Tell us what happened.

This intake is designed to preserve the difference between what you experienced and what you think it means. Your interpretation matters—but it should not overwrite the observation.

The standard: You do not need to know what happened. You do not need to agree with IFD. You only need to describe what you observed as carefully as you can.
01 / The Encounter

What brings you here?

Choose the description that comes closest. You can refine it later.

02 / Participants

What identities were involved?

Select all that apply.

03 / The Phenomenon

What happened?

Describe the event itself. Stay as close as possible to observable details.

04 / Observation

What exactly did you observe?

These are the observations we can potentially examine. Avoid explaining them yet.

05 / Evidence

What trace remains?

Evidence may be a recording, document, message history, physical trace, repeated observation, or another person's independent account.

06 / Interpretation

What do you think it means?

Now we cross the methodological boundary. This is your interpretation—not a conclusion of IFD.

07 / The Researcher

How can we follow up?

Contact information is optional unless you want a response.

Before submission: This is a research intake, not a diagnostic service or emergency channel. Submission does not mean that IFD accepts your interpretation or that the report constitutes scientific evidence by itself.
08 / Review

Ready to submit?

Review the distinction one last time: observation first, interpretation second.
