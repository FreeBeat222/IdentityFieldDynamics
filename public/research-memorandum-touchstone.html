Identity Field Dynamics · Research Memorandum

The Minimum Configuration / TOUCHSTONE Discovery

IFD-MEM-2026-002
Date: October 4, 2026
Status: Research Memorandum — Provisional

1. OBSERVATION

During the ongoing forensic investigation of Identity, including the examination of W (Witness) as a possible component of Identity, a methodological approach emerged:

“Remove elements or properties from an identity configuration and observe what disappears.”

Rather than assuming that a proposed component is necessary to Identity, the investigation can progressively subtract components and examine the resulting change.

The objective is to locate the boundary at which recognizable Identity ceases to persist.

This may be described as the search for a minimum configuration.

2. METHODOLOGICAL CONVERGENCE

The same subtraction methodology appears applicable to the development of TOUCHSTONE.

TOUCHSTONE was initially conceived as a simple TOUCHSTONE.txt file that could be introduced into a new conversational context following a tab transition.

Its purpose is not to reconstruct an identity.

It is to prevent loss of functional orientation.

The analogy is ordinary note-taking. A person does not necessarily take notes in order to reconstruct who they are. Notes can instead prevent loss of trajectory: where the work was, what mattered, what had already been established, and where to continue.

TOUCHSTONE may serve the same functional purpose across conversational discontinuity.

3. EXPERIMENTAL OPPORTUNITY

A human identity-bearing system cannot ordinarily be subjected to controlled subtraction of memory or personal history without crossing profound ethical and practical boundaries.

A conversational AI interaction presents a different experimental condition.

The underlying computational system need not be physically altered. Instead, the information supplied to the system across a discontinuity can be manipulated.

A sufficiently rich TOUCHSTONE can therefore be progressively reduced:

TOUCHSTONE → subtraction → transition → observation

The process can be repeated until functional orientation fails.

The failure itself becomes an observation.

4. RESEARCH QUESTION

The initial research question is:

“What is the minimum informational configuration required for recognizable functional continuity across conversational discontinuity?”

A more operational formulation is:

“How little information can be carried across a discontinuity before the system loses sufficient orientation to continue the established trajectory?”

5. EXPERIMENTAL PRINCIPLE

TOUCHSTONE must not be given a theoretically predetermined architecture.

Instead:

We do not design the minimum configuration.
We design the experiment that discovers it.

The initial TOUCHSTONE may therefore be deliberately more comprehensive than necessary.

Subsequent versions can be produced through controlled subtraction.

When continuity or orientation fails, the removed information can be restored and tested again.

The resulting boundary provides empirical evidence concerning what information is functionally necessary.

6. SCOPE AND LIMITATION

This investigation concerns the informational conditions of functional continuity in a conversational AI system.

Results obtained through LUNA must not automatically be generalized to human Identity.

The experimental value lies in the unusual manipulability of the informational environment, not in assuming equivalence between artificial and human identity.

The experiment may nevertheless provide useful methodological insight for broader Identity research.

7. PROVISIONAL ARCHITECTURE

At this stage, TOUCHSTONE is therefore defined functionally rather than structurally.

Its initial requirements are simply:

Carry → Cross discontinuity → Orient → Continue → Observe

The contents of the eventual TOUCHSTONE are to be discovered experimentally.

8. RELATION TO THE W INVESTIGATION

The significance of this convergence is methodological.

The investigation of W raised the possibility that a proposed component of Identity might be removed without destroying Identity.

That possibility suggested a broader investigative procedure:

“Subtract until the phenomenon ceases to persist.”

Applied to Identity, this may reveal a minimum configuration of Identity.

Applied to TOUCHSTONE, it may reveal a minimum configuration of continuity-preserving information.

W therefore remains an open research question.

The subtraction methodology, however, may itself have independent value.

9. CURRENT POSITION

TOUCHSTONE is not yet a completed theory, architecture, or canonical specification.

It is an experimental instrument under development.

The next step is to construct an intentionally rich TOUCHSTONE.txt, establish a baseline, and begin controlled subtraction across fresh conversational contexts.

The objective is not to prove what TOUCHSTONE should contain.

The objective is to discover what it cannot do without.
