Identity in Encounter.

Identities interact. They respond. They adapt. They leave traces.

The encounter is not merely an event between identities. It changes the conditions under which the next event occurs.

Follow what happens.

INTERACTION

Identity is examined in relation, not only in isolation.

RESPONSE

A response may alter the circumstances of the next encounter.

TRACE

Interaction leaves a record that can become part of history and inform later interpretation.

Begin without deciding in advance what identity must be. Ask what persists, what changes, what differentiates one identity from another, and what occurs when identities encounter each other. Distinguish what was observed from what is inferred.

Human identity may be one instance of identity. Electrical identity may provide another point of observation—not because equivalence is assumed, but because difference can be informative.

IDENTITY → ACTION → INTERACTION → SYNERGY → TRACE

The sequence is a working lens for following how identities act, encounter one another, and leave consequences. It should guide observation, not predetermine the interpretation.
