Tools & Methodologies

IFD uses distinct investigative methods for distinct tasks. These methodologies are not interchangeable: each provides a different way to examine structure, identify weaknesses, or observe phenomena. Their claims and results remain open to examination. The LUNATIC STANDARD™ establishes the common discipline of scrutiny applied across the entire investigative enterprise—including the Standard itself.

THE LUNATIC STANDARD™ — Mother of All Tools

Governing meta-standard · Recursive rigor

The LUNATIC STANDARD™ is IFD's governing standard of methodological and evidentiary scrutiny. It is the common examination protocol applied to every IFD construct, instrument, methodology, formulation, inference, and conclusion. It exists to determine what a claim actually says, what would make it assessable, what supports it, where it fails, and what remains unknown.

It is a meta-standard because it does not perform the specialized work of Binary Vision™, OBSERVOR™, or FLAW CHART™. Instead, it examines the definitions, procedures, assumptions, outputs, and limitations of those tools—and of the larger architecture in which they operate. No instrument receives exemption because it is useful, elegant, familiar, or central to IFD.

Seven dimensions of examination

  1. 1. DefinitionWhat exactly is being claimed? Terms, scope, and the claim itself must be sufficiently clear to examine.
  2. 2. OperationalizationHow can the claim or construct be observed, applied, or investigated? What procedure connects the idea to an observable result?
  3. 3. Internal coherenceAre the definitions, assumptions, relationships, and inferences logically consistent? Are contradictions or circular arguments present?
  4. 4. ReproducibilityCan another investigator follow the stated procedure and obtain comparable observations or results under comparable conditions?
  5. 5. FalsifiabilityWhat possible evidence or result would count against the claim? A claim that cannot risk being wrong cannot be presented as empirically established.
  6. 6. Empirical validationWhat has actually been observed, measured, tested, replicated, or independently corroborated? Conceptual appeal is not empirical confirmation.
  7. 7. LimitationsWhat does the method or evidence not establish? What are its boundary conditions, uncertainties, failure modes, and unresolved questions?

Governing principle: The architecture must emerge from the evidence. The evidence must never be distorted to accommodate the architecture.

Rigor is recursive. The LUNATIC STANDARD™ is itself subject to the LUNATIC STANDARD™: its definitions, criteria, practical usefulness, consistency, and limitations must also withstand examination. It cannot claim authority merely by declaring itself the authority.

The LUNATIC STANDARD™ is an IFD-developed methodological framework for disciplined evaluation. Its adoption within IFD does not make it an externally certified or independently validated scientific standard. Its own performance and adequacy remain open to testing and revision.

Binary Vision™

Structural-investigation methodology

Binary Vision™ is an IFD methodology, conceptual and architecturally-structurally based upon the diffractive method used with X-rays to investigate molecular structure, including the structure of DNA.

Its guiding idea is that structure may not be directly apparent from a single view. Examining a subject through two deliberately differentiated perspectives—and studying the patterns and relationships revealed between them—can provide information about underlying organization that either perspective alone may not disclose.

The X-ray diffraction work is the methodological inspiration. Binary Vision™ is an IFD-developed conceptual methodology; its procedures, scope, and evidentiary performance must be evaluated on their own merits.

FLAW CHART™

Weakness identification & prioritization

FLAW CHART™ is an investigative scoreboard for identifying, ranking, and tracking weaknesses, vulnerabilities, and areas requiring inspection, redesign, or better use. Its purpose is to make the most consequential flaws visible and actionable rather than allowing them to disappear into general commentary.

OBSERVOR™

Structured observation

OBSERVOR™ supports disciplined examination of observable phenomena and behavior before assigning identity-level explanations. It separates what is observed from what is inferred, helping preserve the distinction between evidence and interpretation.
