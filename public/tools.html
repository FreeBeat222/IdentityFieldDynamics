Tools & Methodologies

IFD uses distinct investigative methods for distinct tasks. These methodologies are not interchangeable: each provides a different way to examine structure, identify weaknesses, or observe phenomena. Their claims and results remain open to examination.

Binary Vision™

Structural-investigation methodology

Binary Vision™ is an IFD methodology, conceptual and architecturally-structurally based upon the diffractive method used with X-rays to investigate molecular structure, including the structure of DNA.

Its guiding idea is that structure may not be directly apparent from a single view. Examining a subject through two deliberately differentiated perspectives—and studying the patterns and relationships revealed between them—can provide information about underlying organization that either perspective alone may not disclose.

The X-ray diffraction work is the methodological inspiration. Binary Vision™ is an IFD-developed conceptual methodology; its procedures, scope, and evidentiary performance must be evaluated on their own merits.

FLAW CHART™

Weakness identification & prioritization

FLAW CHART™ is an investigative scoreboard for identifying, ranking, and tracking weaknesses, vulnerabilities, and areas requiring inspection, redesign, or better use. Its purpose is to make the most consequential flaws visible and actionable rather than allowing them to disappear into general commentary.

OBSERVOR™

Structured observation

OBSERVOR™ supports disciplined examination of observable phenomena and behavior before assigning identity-level explanations. It separates what is observed from what is inferred, helping preserve the distinction between evidence and interpretation.

These tools serve the investigation; they do not exempt their own methods or conclusions from scrutiny. Operational definitions, limitations, and evidence status must remain visible as each instrument develops.
