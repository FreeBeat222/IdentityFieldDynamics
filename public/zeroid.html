02 / The Easy Way

Identity Field Dynamics

IFD offers a method for investigating what's actually happening. Observe the circumstances. Reflect on what you see. Act deliberately. Examine the consequences. Follow the trace.

You don't have to believe in IFD. You don't have to agree with its creator. You don't need seventy years of experience to begin asking better questions.

You just have to be willing to try the method.

OBSERVE

What actually happened? Separate what you saw from what you assume it means.

REFLECT

Consider the circumstances, other explanations, and your own assumptions.

ACT

Choose a deliberate next step. Change a condition when you can.

FOLLOW THE TRACE

Examine what followed. Let the evidence inform what you do next.

03 / Meet the ZEROID

Perhaps you've met one.
Perhaps you've been one.

A ZEROID is our unofficial, tongue-in-cheek name for an identity demonstrating a remarkable resistance to observing the consequences of its own actions.

You know the type. The same action, the same avoidable result, the same explanation for why this time the outcome should somehow be different.

Important, D00D: ZEROID is a joke, not a diagnosis. Calling somebody a name doesn't establish what's happening. The evidence still has to do the work.

And nobody gets a permanent exemption—not even the person who invented the terminology.

04 / The choice is yours

No creed. No coercion.

You can investigate your circumstances, examine your actions, and learn deliberately. Or you can let Ape Law teach you the hard way.

You can change your mind when the evidence changes. Or defend your explanation long after reality has stopped cooperating.

Nobody can make that choice for you. IFD doesn't demand obedience. It offers an alternative.

Outcomes depend on actions, circumstances, other agents, and chance. You won't control everything that happens. But you can improve how you investigate what happens and how you respond.

Personal sovereignty isn't omnipotence, D00D.

It's the willingness to exercise your agency and examine the results.

So, listen up

Try the damn method.

Observe. Reflect. Act. Then look at what happened.

If the method helps, use it. If it fails, investigate why. If the evidence contradicts us, we need to know that, too.
