## 3. Evidence and status vocabulary

Use one of these labels for each claim or record:

- **VERIFIED PRIMARY RECORD:** Source page/document directly inspected and its identifying details captured.
- **VERIFIED ARCHIVAL CAPTURE:** Dated archive or repository snapshot inspected; capture date is explicit.
- **CATALOG DATE ONLY:** Platform displays a date, but earliest availability/version history has not been established.
- **SEARCH-SNIPPET LEAD:** Only a search result/snippet is available; do not treat it as source text.
- **PROJECT SUMMARY:** A project note reports a past event/search, but the original record has not been recovered.
- **INFERENCE:** Interpretation from available facts; state alternatives and uncertainty.
- **UNRESOLVED:** Current evidence does not decide the question.

Every new entry should record: exact title; author as credited; stable URL/DOI/repository ID; source/platform; displayed date; first verified public-availability evidence; version history; date we inspected it; exact overlap (with quotations only when checked); differences; and unresolved questions.

## 4. Questions to keep separate

1. **Terminology:** Is the same phrase used, and in what sense?
2. **Conceptual overlap:** Are constructs or methods meaningfully similar, beyond shared vocabulary?
3. **Chronology:** What is the earliest independently verified public availability of each version?
4. **Influence:** Is there affirmative evidence of access, citation, acknowledgment, or dependence?
5. **Record integrity:** Is there evidence of a changed date, hidden revision, or false chronology?
6. **Originality:** Which specific formulation or method can be shown in a dated IFD artifact, and what earlier work must be compared against it?

Do not use a positive answer to one question as a substitute for evidence on another.
