## Experimental interpretation

The sequence is a record of generated visual responses to successive viewpoint instructions. Its research value lies in preserving the ordered outputs so that environmental continuity and perspective-dependent variation can be compared across the sequence.

The record supports the existence of four recovered visual TRACE files in the stated order. It does not, by itself, establish that the system maintained an independently persistent external world, possessed human-like spatial awareness, or generated the views through a particular internal mechanism. Those are separate questions requiring additional evidence.
